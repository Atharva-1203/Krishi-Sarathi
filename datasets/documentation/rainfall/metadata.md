# Rainfall Database Metadata

- **Source Website**: Maharashtra Government Rainfall Recording Portal (Maharain)
- **URL**: https://maharain.maharashtra.gov.in/test/maharain/previous_year_rain.php
- **Extraction Date**: 2026-07-31
- **Available Years**: 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 (11 completed historical years).
- **Geographical Divisions**: Konkan, Nashik, Pune, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Amravati, Nagpur (6 divisions).
- **Geographical Districts**: 34 agricultural districts. (Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban are excluded due to lack of agricultural land).
- **Seasons Represented**: Monsoon (accumulated June to September monthly readings).
- **Measurement Units**: Rainfall values are reported in millimeters (mm); departure percentages are in %.
- **Measurement Method**: Calculated from daily telemetric rain gauge (TRG) networks installed in agricultural circles (sub-district units), aggregated to Tehsil and District averages.
- **Portal Limitations**:
  - The raw HTML output generated by the server has major layout syntax bugs (such as missing `<tr>` opening tags and `<dth>` closing tags).
  - The database uses local district spellings (`Raigadh`, `Parabhani`) and does not dynamically map standard Local Government Directory (LGD) or Census codes.
