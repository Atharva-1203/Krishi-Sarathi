# Land Use Planning Metadata

- **Thematic Layers on Portal**:
  - Crop Suitability (Rice, Wheat, Sorghum, Cotton, Sugarcane, Soybean, Grapes, Oil Palm etc.)
  - Land Management Units (LMUs - derived from Soil-Landscape mapping)
  - Prime Agricultural Lands (PAL) - subdivided into Rainfed and Irrigated zones.
- **Delineation Scale**: 1:250,000 district-level maps.
- **Suitability Criteria**: FAO Land Evaluation Framework, mapping soil attributes (depth, texture, pH, drainage) and climate attributes (rainfall, temperature) to crop requirements.
