# Physiography Metadata

- **Source Models**: SRTM 90m DEM, SRTM 30m DEM, ALOS 12.5m DEM, India Hillshade Layer.
- **Thematic Layers on Portal**:
  - Major Physiographic Regions (`Major_Physiographic_Regions_DistWise`)
  - Sub Physiographic Regions (`Sub_Physiographic_Regions_DistWise`)
  - Broad Landforms (`Broad_Landforms_DistWise`)
- **Spatial Resolution**: Originally derived from raster DEM models, vectorized at 1:250,000 scale.
- **Update Frequency**: Static geological and terrain formations.
