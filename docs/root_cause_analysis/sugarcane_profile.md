# Sugarcane Agronomic Feature Profile

Compared to other crops in the Pune Division dataset:
- **Nitrogen (N)**: Mean is 131.6 kg/ha (Cotton is 117.6, Wheat is 91.7).
- **Phosphorus (P)**: Mean is 70.6 kg/ha.
- **Potassium (K)**: Mean is 113.98 kg/ha.
- **Rainfall**: Mean is 1032.3 mm (Min is 700mm in Sangli).

### Correlation with Target:
- **Potassium (K) Correlation**: **0.7581** (extremely high positive correlation with Sugarcane class!).
- **Nitrogen (N) Correlation**: **0.5108**
- **Phosphorus (P) Correlation**: **0.5284**
