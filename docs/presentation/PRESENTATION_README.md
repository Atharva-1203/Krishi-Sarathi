# 🏆 Final Hackathon Presentation & Walkthrough Guide

## Presentation Positioning
> **KRISHI SARATHI**
> *AI-Powered Agricultural Decision Intelligence Platform*
> **Predict. Explain. Simulate. Evaluate Risk. Optimize Profit. Decide.**

## 10-Step Live Demo Flow
1. **Landing Page**: Introduce telemetry (81,713 ML rows, 779K Soil Cards, 785.8K Evidence Base).
2. **Agronomic Input**: Enter soil NPK and climate parameters.
3. **ML Prediction**: Display Top-5 suitability recommendations (ExtraTrees 98.86% / 96.76%).
4. **Explainability**: Open "Why this crop?" reasoning and limiting parameter analysis.
5. **What-If Simulator**: Perturb rainfall slider and demonstrate probability shift.
6. **Profit Intelligence**: Show suitability vs profit vs water vs risk table.
7. **Strategic Callout**: Demonstrate **Highest Suitability $
eq$ Highest Economic Return**.
8. **Maharashtra Observatory**: Show district soil chemistry, climate trends, Shannon diversity.
9. **Farm Digital Twin**: Present unified farm profile.
10. **PDF Export**: Generate 7-Page Farm Intelligence Advisory Report.
