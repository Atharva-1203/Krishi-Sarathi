# Data Provenance Report

This report outlines the data origins, providers, licenses, and designated usage of all datasets.

---

## 1. Provenance Registry

### V3.1 Predictor Training Set
- **Provider**: Public Agricultural Research Benchmarks (UCI / Kaggle repository).
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Purpose**: Trained the active Extremely Randomized Trees classifier.
- **Designation**: **TRAINING & VALIDATION**.

### Maharashtra Soil Health Card Database
- **Provider**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
- **License**: Government Open Data License (GODL) India.
- **Purpose**: Calibrates regional soil averages and maps Soil Quality Indices.
- **Designation**: **MAP & ANALYTICS**.

### Labeled Crop-Fertilizer Survey
- **Provider**: Regional Agricultural Universities Survey.
- **License**: Academic Research Use.
- **Purpose**: Evaluated for training candidate models.
- **Designation**: **REJECTED (No Humidity feature)**.

### IMD Gridded Rainfall
- **Provider**: Indian Meteorological Department (IMD), Pune.
- **License**: Meteorological Research License.
- **Purpose**: Maps monthly rainfall precipitation series and departures.
- **Designation**: **MAP & ANALYTICS**.
