# External Dataset Catalog

This document registers official public agricultural and meteorological databases.

---

## 1. Meteorological Registries

### A. Indian Meteorological Department (IMD)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India.
- **Dataset Name**: Daily/Monthly Gridded Rainfall & Temperature Product.
- **URL**: [https://imdpune.gov.in](https://imdpune.gov.in)
- **License**: Non-commercial research use.
- **Coverage**: National (1901–present).
- **Features**: Gridded precipitation ($0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$ resolution) and temperature ($1.0^\circ \times 1.0^\circ$).
- **Crop Labels**: None.
- **Compatibility**: High (for visual map charts only).

---

## 2. Agricultural Registries

### B. Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
- **Dataset Name**: Area, Production & Yield (APY) Statistics.
- **URL**: [https://desagri.gov.in](https://desagri.gov.in)
- **License**: Government Open Data License - India (GODL).
- **Coverage**: National, district-wise (1997–present).
- **Features**: Crop area (ha), Production (tonnes), Yield (kg/ha).
- **Crop Labels**: Yes (district crop yields).
- **Compatibility**: High (for visual map charts only).

### C. National Open Government Data Portal (data.gov.in)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Electronics & IT, Government of India.
- **Dataset Name**: Soil Health Card registrations.
- **URL**: [https://data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in)
- **License**: GODL India.
- **Coverage**: National (cycles 1–6).
- **Features**: Macronutrients, pH, EC, micronutrients.
- **Crop Labels**: None.
- **Compatibility**: High (powers Soil Quality Index averages on the GIS map).
