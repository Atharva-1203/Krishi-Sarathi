# V4 Data Provenance Report

This document records the original sources, publishers, licenses, and collection methodologies of all data assets.

---

## 1. Provenance Registry

### A. Crop Recommendation Dataset (2,200 Rows)
- **Source**: Kaggle Crop Recommendation Benchmark.
- **Original Publisher**: Supplementary data from peer-reviewed agricultural journals on multiclass crop classification.
- **Collector**: Academic researchers collecting field readings across diverse Indian agro-climatic zones.
- **License**: Public Domain / Creative Commons.

### B. Maharashtra Soil Health Card Database (779,144 Rows)
- **Source**: Department of Agriculture, Government of Maharashtra.
- **Original Publisher**: soilhealth.dac.gov.in (National Soil Health Card Scheme).
- **Collector**: Block-level agricultural field functionaries taking composite soil samples at grid points.
- **License**: Govt Open Data License (GODL) India.

### C. Labeled Crop-Fertilizer Survey (4,513 Rows)
- **Source**: Maharashtra agricultural extension surveys.
- **Original Publisher**: Regional agricultural university supplementary records.
- **Collector**: Local surveys mapping crop choices, fertilizer dosing, and soil profiles.
- **License**: Open for academic/hackathon study.
