# Farm Digital Twin Framework (V5 Final Evolution)

This document describes the design and indexes of the digital twin farm visual representation.

---

## 1. Digital Twin Profile
The Farm Digital Twin aggregates raw inputs and model probabilities into five visual gauges:
1.  **Farm Intelligence Score**: A weighted index representing the overall crop-soil-climate fit.
2.  **Soil Compatibility**: Profile alignment score across N, P, K, pH.
3.  **Climate Compatibility**: Fit against local temperature and humidity.
4.  **Water Compatibility**: Suitability index matching crop transpiration needs with annual rainfall patterns.
5.  **Climate Risk Index**: Outlines threat levels from temperature spikes or drought anomalies.
