# Final Innovation Report (V5 Final Evolution)

This document highlights the key product differentiators implemented on the platform.

---

## 1. Differentiating Innovations
1.  **Farm Digital Twin**: Contextualizes farm soil, water, and climate parameters into visual gauges and an overall intelligence score.
2.  **What-If Scenario Simulator**: Instant predictions based on slider perturbations ($\pm 20\%$) and highlights parameter sensitivity.
3.  **Out-of-Distribution Rejection**: Boundary percentile checks to flag caution states and refuse physically impossible entries.
4.  **Calibrated Crop Ranking**: Sigmoid Platt-calibrated top 5 recommendations.
5.  **Explainability Medians**: Soil medians match scorecards detailing supporting parameters and limiting factors.
