# Data Source Registry (V5 Final Evolution)

This registry records the specifications, provenance, licenses, and scopes of all agricultural data assets integrated into Krishi Sarathi.

---

## 1. Registry Catalog

### A. Balanced Crop Suitability Dataset (V3.1)
- **Source URL**: [UCI / Kaggle Benchmarks](https://kaggle.com)
- **Organization**: Public Agricultural Research Repositories
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Sample Count**: 2,200 observations
- **Target Availability**: 22 balanced crop suitability classes (100 samples per class)
- **Spatial Scope**: Location-blind continuous agronomic features
- **Usage**: Predictor training and stratified cross-validation

### B. Maharashtra Soil Health Card Database
- **Source URL**: [Soil Health Card Portal](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in)
- **Organization**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture, Govt of India
- **License**: Government Open Data License (GODL) India
- **Sample Count**: 779,144 observations
- **Target Availability**: None (Observational soil chemistry records only)
- **Spatial Scope**: 34 districts of Maharashtra state
- **Usage**: Maharashtra GIS Observatory mapping and similarity retrieval

### C. IMD Gridded Rainfall Records
- **Source URL**: [IMD Pune Gridded Meteorological Data Portal](https://imdpune.gov.in)
- **Organization**: Indian Meteorological Department (IMD), Pune
- **License**: Meteorological Research and Open Use
- **Sample Count**: 11-year monthly gridded precipitation grids ($0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$)
- **Usage**: Map layer climate trend analytics (2015-2025)

### D. DES Agricultural Yield Statistics
- **Source URL**: [Directorate of Economics & Statistics APY Portal](https://desagri.gov.in)
- **Organization**: Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Ministry of Agriculture, Govt of India
- **License**: Government Open Data License (GODL) India
- **Sample Count**: District-wise annual crop production area and yield matrices
- **Usage**: Map layer agricultural yield trends and Shannon crop diversity calculations
