# Climate Risk & Volatility Engine (V5 Final Evolution)

This document describes the calculations of the crop-specific risk engine.

---

## 1. Risk Components
- **Rainfall Risk**: Compares current crop rainfall requirements against local IMD historical distributions to warn about moisture stress.
- **Temperature Risk**: Detects thermal limits to warn about heat and cold shock limits.
- **Overall Risk Index**: Combined agricultural stability index showing LOW, MODERATE, or HIGH risk.
