# Final Round Map Story (V5 Final Evolution)

This document maps out our final round GIS Map observatory narrative.

---

## 1. Narration Keypoints
- The interactive Maharashtra map serves as our decoupled agricultural database observatory.
- Selecting a district renders the District Intelligence Card:
  - Average Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Organic Carbon, pH, and EC.
  - Soil quality index values.
  - Crop diversity index graphs.
  - Precipitation anomalies (2015-2025).
  - Crop yield bar charts.
- Highlights: Combines IMD, DES, and Soil Health Cards into a cohesive visual intelligence layer.
