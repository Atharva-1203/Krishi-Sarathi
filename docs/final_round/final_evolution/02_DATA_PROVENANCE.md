# Data Provenance Report (V5 Final Evolution)

This report logs the official source registries, providers, licenses, and designated system functions of all data assets.

---

## 1. Provenance Registry

### V3.1 Predictor training dataset
- **Provider**: Public Agricultural Research Repositories (UCI / Kaggle)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Application**: Supervised learning and model validation.

### Maharashtra Soil Health Cards
- **Provider**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **License**: Government Open Data License (GODL) India
- **Application**: Observational soil intelligence and GIS observatory.

### IMD Gridded Rainfall anomalies
- **Provider**: Indian Meteorological Department (IMD), Pune
- **License**: Open Meteorological Use
- **Application**: Climate observatory and gridded precipitation trends.

### DES Crop Production registries
- **Provider**: Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Govt of India
- **License**: Government Open Data License (GODL) India
- **Application**: District agricultural statistics, crop yields, and diversity indexing.
