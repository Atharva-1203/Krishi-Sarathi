# GIS Agricultural Observatory (V5 Final Evolution)

This document describes the mapping and district agricultural layers integrated from the 7.9L database.

---

## 1. Integrated Observational Layers
- **Soil Quality Index**: Weighted combination of N, P, K, pH, Organic Carbon, and EC.
- **Gridded Rainfall Anomalies**: Departure from IMD normal precipitation boundaries.
- **Agricultural Production (DES)**: Crop area, total production, and average yields.
- **Shannon Crop Diversity Index**: 
  \[H = -\sum p_i \ln p_i\]
  Measures the species diversity of district crop yields.
- **HHI Concentration Index**:
  \[HHI = \sum s_i^2\]
  Identifies monoculture risk profiles.
