## 2. Decision Rationale
1.  **Generalization Collapse**: Training on the merged dataset causes spatial memorization, leading to a performance collapse on unseen regions.
2.  **Imputation Noise**: The V5 candidate relies on proxy-imputed humidity, introducing synthetic correlations.
3.  **Sugarcane Bias**: The V5 model recommends sugarcane in $24.5\%$ of randomized tests.

We recommend **retaining V3.1 as the active production champion**.
