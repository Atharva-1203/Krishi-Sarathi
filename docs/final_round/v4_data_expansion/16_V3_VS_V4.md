## 2. Decision Rationale
1.  **Imputation Noise**: The V4 model relies on proxy humidity values, leading to artificial decision tree splits.
2.  **Sugarcane Bias**: The V4 candidate re-introduces the sugarcane dominance issue due to class imbalance.
3.  **Generalization Collapse**: V4 suffers from spatial memorization leakage, causing a performance collapse on unseen regions.

We recommend **retaining V3.1 as the active production champion**.
