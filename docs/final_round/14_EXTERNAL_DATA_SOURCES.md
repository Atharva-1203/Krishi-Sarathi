# Krishi Sarathi: External Official Data Registries

This document logs verified external government registries selected to support the platform.

---

## 1. Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt of India.
- **Exposure**: Crop area, production volumes, yield statistics.
- **Granularity**: District-wise, Annual.
- **License**: Government Open Data License - India (GODL).
- **Role**: Map crop production trend graphs.

---

## 2. Indian Meteorological Department (IMD)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Earth Sciences, Govt of India.
- **Exposure**: Gridded daily/monthly rainfall and temperature anomalies.
- **Granularity**: $0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$ grid cells aggregated to district bounds.
- **License**: Non-commercial academic research.
- **Role**: Historical rain deviation charts and drought risk flags.

---

## 3. Data.gov.in (National Open Data Portal)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Electronics & IT, Govt of India.
- **Exposure**: Aggregated agricultural yield survey records.
- **License**: GODL India.
- **Role**: Soil Health Card summaries index.
