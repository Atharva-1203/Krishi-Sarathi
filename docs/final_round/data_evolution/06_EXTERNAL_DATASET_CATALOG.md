# Krishi Sarathi: External Dataset Catalog

This document registers official datasets evaluated for spatial and climatological data expansion.

---

## 1. Verified Official Registries

### A. Indian Meteorological Department (IMD)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India.
- **Exposure**: Daily and monthly gridded temperature and precipitation grids.
- **Granularity**: $0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$ resolution.
- **Temporal Range**: 1901–present.
- **Crop Labels**: None.
- **Relevance**: Rainfall averages and departures mapped on the GIS observatory.

### B. Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
- **Exposure**: Annual district-wise crop area, production, and yield estimates.
- **Granularity**: District-level.
- **Temporal Range**: 1997–present.
- **Crop Labels**: Yes (Crop category yields).
- **Relevance**: Crop diversification timelines on map displays.

### C. Soil Health Card Portal (data.gov.in)
- **Organization**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
- **Exposure**: Soil Health Card registrations (macronutrients, micronutrients, pH, EC).
- **Granularity**: National (village-level test points).
- **Temporal Range**: Cycles 1–6 (2015–2022).
- **Crop Labels**: **None** (soil chemistry readings only).
- **Relevance**: Populates the decoupled average Soil Quality Index (SQI) choropleth layers.
