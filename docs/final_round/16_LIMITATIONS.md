# Krishi Sarathi: Platform Limitations

This document lists the limitations of the current Krishi Sarathi platform.

---

## 1. Agronomic Limitations
- **Soil parameters only**: Recommendations are based on 7 core features. They do not factor in dynamic soil variables like microbial content, organic matter variations, trace element deficiencies, or local weed/disease infestations.
- **Yield advisory only**: Evaluates physiological suitability; does not guarantee crop yield or farming output.

---

## 2. Meteorological & Climatic Limitations
- **Seasonal climatology**: Uses seasonal parameters (like average temperature and rainfall). It does not integrate real-time 3-day weather alerts or sudden storm predictions.

---

## 3. Economic Limitations
- **No market tracking**: Recommends crops based on soil suitability, not current market crop prices, seed logistics, or transportation costs.
- **Water access assumptions**: Recommends water-intensive crops (like rice) based on average rainfall thresholds, assuming irrigation access is available if rainfall targets are met.
