# Chapter 5: Prediction Diversity Audit

1,000 synthetic simulations covering Maharashtra agricultural inputs were run to compare prediction diversity:
- **V1 Baseline (No sample weights)**: Highly concentrated. Cotton (8), Chickpea (0), Masoor (0).
- **V2 Balanced**: Diversity indices increased. Cotton counts rose to 33, Chickpea to 10, and Masoor rose to 30.
- **Conclusion**: Balanced RF yields a broader and fairer representation of minor crops.
