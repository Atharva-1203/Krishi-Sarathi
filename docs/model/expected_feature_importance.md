# Expected Feature Importance Hypothesis

Without training models, this report hypothesizes feature importances based on crop physiology.

## 1. Predicted Top 10 Features

1. **Rainfall**: Most critical feature. Water availability splits dryland crops (Millets, Chickpea) from wetland crops (Rice, Sugarcane).
2. **pH**: Direct soil chemistry constraint. Controls nutrient availability.
3. **K (Potassium)**: Governs crop water-stress tolerance and root strength.
4. **N (Nitrogen)**: Governs leafy vegetative growth.
5. **Temperature**: Determines thermal limits for winter (Rabi) vs summer (Kharif) crops.
6. **Soil Texture**: Governs water retention (e.g. Clayey soil is vital for Sugarcane/Rice).
7. **P (Phosphorus)**: Governs early root development.
8. **Humidity**: Controls transpiration rates.
9. **Organic Carbon (OC)**: Overall indicator of organic matter health.
10. **Soil Depth**: Dictates root volume capacity.
