# 🗺️ Maharashtra Agricultural Observatory

## Regional Spatial Intelligence
The Agricultural Observatory integrates 779,144 Soil Health Cards with IMD climate series and DES yield statistics across all 34 districts of Maharashtra:

- **Soil Health Mapping**: District-level mean and median distribution for N, P, K, pH, Organic Carbon, and Electrical Conductivity.
- **Climate Analytics**: 11-year seasonal rainfall trends (2015–2025), rainfall departure percentages, and drought risk indicators.
- **Shannon Crop Diversity Index**: $H = -\sum p_i \ln p_i$ measuring agricultural crop diversity per district.
- **Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)**: $HHI = \sum s_i^2$ measuring crop monoculture concentration.
