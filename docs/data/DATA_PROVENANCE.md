# 🔍 Data Provenance & Source Matrix

Every dataset in Krishi Sarathi is backed by official published government and research sources:

1. **ICAR / FAO Crop Physiology Corpus**:
   - Authority: Indian Council of Agricultural Research & UN FAO
   - Scope: Controlled agronomic physiology trials for 22 major crop species.
2. **Soil Health Cards Database**:
   - Authority: Department of Agriculture, Govt of Maharashtra (`soilhealth.dac.gov.in`)
   - Records: 779,144 verified lab soil sample test cards across 34 districts.
3. **IMD Climate Grid**:
   - Authority: India Meteorological Department (`mausam.imd.gov.in`)
   - Data: High-resolution 0.25° gridded daily rainfall and temperature observations.
4. **DES Agricultural Statistics**:
   - Authority: Directorate of Economics & Statistics (`eands.dacnet.nic.in`)
   - Data: Cost of cultivation, crop area, production, and yield estimates.
5. **AGMARKNET Mandi Price Benchmarks**:
   - Authority: Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (`agmarknet.gov.in`)
   - Data: Wholesale price benchmarks and price volatility indices (2023-2025).
